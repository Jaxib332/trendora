AI COPILOT · GOOGLE FLOW READY

Hooks, character & Flow video prompts

Describe your character in your own words. You get scroll-stopping hooks, a locked character sheet, image prompts for Flow Ingredients, and shot-by-shot Veo prompts with camera, audio and dialogue.

How to use this in Google Flow
  1. Make the character reference images first with the Ingredients prompts, in Flow or Imagen. Keep the one that looks most like your character.
  2. Start a Flow project, choose Ingredients to Video, and add that image so she looks the same in every shot.
  3. Paste one scene prompt at a time. Each Veo clip is about 8 seconds, so one scene is one clip.
  4. The character sheet is repeated word for word in every scene. Don't edit it between scenes, that's what keeps her consistent.
  5. Line the clips up in Scene Builder. Use Extend when a shot needs to continue the same action.
  6. Dialogue goes in quotes. Keep each line short enough to say in about 6 seconds.
  7. Add captions and on-screen text in your editor afterwards. Video models often misspell text.
